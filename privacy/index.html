Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026.

Rainnight Labs builds software and digital products with a preference for minimal data collection. This policy explains how information may be handled on rainnightlabs.com and in Rainnight Labs products.

Website data

Basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and aggregate usage information may be processed by our hosting or analytics providers for security and service operation.

Purchases

Payments will be processed by our payment provider. Rainnight Labs does not directly store complete payment-card details. Transaction information such as email address, product, payment status, and transaction identifiers may be retained to provide purchases, licenses, refunds, and support.

Browser extension data

List2Sheet is designed to perform its core extraction work locally in the browser where practical. The final extension listing will disclose its exact permissions and data practices before public release.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to admin@rainnightlabs.com.
